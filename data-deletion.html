Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: [TO BE COMPLETED] · Source review: October 22, 2026

Draft: a working request channel and an operational deletion procedure must be established before this page is used for Meta review.

1. Who can request deletion

Internal users and people whose information appears in Page interactions can request deletion of information held by enterprise-test about them. The operator is Feng Siyang, an individual developer based in Guangdong, China.

2. Send a request

  1. Contact lei1294192563@163.com. Use the subject enterprise-test — Data Deletion Request.
  2. Describe the data you want removed. Where available, include the relevant Facebook Page name or ID and the HTTPS link or ID of your post or comment. Internal users may include their work email address. If you do not have these identifiers, provide enough context to locate your record.
  3. Provide a reply address. The operator may need proportionate verification that the request concerns you or a Page you are authorized to manage. Do not send your Facebook password, App Secret, API key or access token.

Feng Siyang handles requests personally and acknowledges receipt within 7 days. After proportionate identity and data-scope verification, requests are normally completed within 30 days, or sooner where applicable law requires. Reply to the same email to ask about status. No automated confirmation number is provided.

3. Data to be assessed

Depending on the request, the operator needs to locate relevant Page credentials and pending Page selections, cached Meta responses, imported posts and comments, reply records, internal assignments and notes, metrics, uploaded media, employee/session records, publishing and synchronization jobs, API usage and audit records. Historical backups and exported copies must also be assessed.

The present application has no complete self-service purge or automated Meta data deletion callback. Deletion requires an operator-managed procedure. Feng Siyang is responsible for the manual deletion procedure. Backups are subject to an operator-approved maximum retention period of 180 days from creation; this is a policy commitment, not a claim of automatic software deletion. Relevant exported copies are also assessed. [TO BE COMPLETED: implement and verify full manual deletion, backup expiry and any specific lawful retention exceptions].

4. Disconnecting the Page

An internal administrator can open the application's account settings and select the Page's disconnect action (the current interface labels this action “断开”). It clears the account's stored access credentials, cache and OAuth states and cancels queued publishing jobs. If publishing is already in progress, disconnection is blocked until the operation finishes.

This action retains the account record, historical posts, comments, replies, metrics and audit or operational history. It is not a full deletion request. Use the contact channel above to request deletion of remaining records.

5. Removing authorization through Meta

You can also remove the application's authorization using the Facebook settings area where connected apps or business integrations are listed. Menu names vary by account and Facebook interface. Review Business Integrations or Apps and Websites as applicable.

Removing authorization does not automatically delete data already stored in enterprise-test. Submit a separate request for that data. If the Page is reconnected or synchronization continues, available Facebook content may be imported again; the operator must address that risk when carrying out the request.

6. Content on Facebook and completion

Deleting application-held records does not by itself remove a post, comment or reply from Facebook. Manage Facebook-hosted content using Facebook's own controls and applicable permissions. Meta controls the information retained by Facebook under its own policies.

The operator replies by email to confirm the outcome and explain any retained records and their reasons. If individual records cannot be removed from a backup immediately, access is restricted and the records are excluded from business use until backup expiry, no later than 180 days from its creation. Deleted records must be removed again before restored data is used. [TO BE COMPLETED: verify backup cleanup and restore-time deletion procedures].
