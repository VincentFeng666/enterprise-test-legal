Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Send a request

Email lei1294192563@163.com with the subject enterprise-test — Data Deletion Request.

Identify the information concerning you that you want removed. If available, include the relevant platform and account or Page name/ID, a post or comment link/ID, or your internal account email. For private messages, provide your platform username and approximate conversation date/time; provide a message or conversation ID only if available. If you do not have these identifiers, describe enough context to help locate your record. Include an address for replies. You do not need an internal application account to submit a request.

Do not send passwords, App Secrets, API keys or access tokens. The operator may request proportionate information to verify that the data concerns you or that you have authority to request deletion for the relevant Page. Information supplied for verification will be used for handling the request.

2. Response and processing

Feng Siyang, the individual developer based in Guangdong, China, handles requests personally. Receipt will be acknowledged within 7 days. After identity and data-scope verification, requests will normally be completed within 30 days, subject to any shorter applicable legal deadline. Email the same address to ask about status.

The operator will assess relevant credentials, cached responses, posts, comments, replies, notes, assignments, metrics, uploaded media, internal account records and related operational records. This includes Facebook Messenger and Instagram conversations, message bodies and participant identifiers, raw received event records and queued/retry processing work. Platform media metadata, exported reports and relevant downloaded copies are also assessed. Relevant exported copies and backups will also be assessed. The operator pauses affected history synchronization and incoming-event processing before cleanup and checks that delayed events or retries cannot recreate the deleted records. Unrelated participants' data is not removed by a blanket account deletion. The process is manual; enterprise-test does not provide a complete automated self-service deletion feature or Meta deletion callback.

The operator will reply with the outcome and explain any information that must be retained, its reason and period, unless legally prohibited. Only necessary information required by applicable law will be retained as an exception.

3. Backups and reimport

Backups have a maximum retention period of 180 days from creation. Where selective deletion is not feasible, affected backup information will be excluded from ordinary business use until expiry. Deleted information must be removed again before a restored backup is used. The operator will address history synchronization, new or delayed Webhook events, retries and restored processing queues so that deleted comments or messages are not simply imported again. Where reliable exclusion is not available, affected synchronization or event processing remains paused. Requests received after a backup was created must be reapplied to its conversations, messages, event records and related copies before the restored environment is put into use.

The 180-day backup limit does not mean active records are kept for 180 days after a deletion request. Active records are handled within the request process described above. Backup expiry and restoration safeguards require operator-managed procedures.

4. Disconnecting and removing authorization

An internal administrator can use the Page's disconnect action in account settings. It removes stored Page credentials and cancels queued publishing work but retains historical application records. If publishing is in progress, disconnection may be blocked until it finishes. Submit a deletion request to remove remaining information.

You can also remove enterprise-test authorization through the relevant platform's connected-app settings. For Facebook, review connected apps or business integrations as applicable:

  • https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools
  • https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications

For Instagram and X, review the account's connected-app or website-permission settings. Revocation and local cleanup are separate actions. Removing authorization does not automatically delete application history. Deleting application-held records does not remove posts, comments, replies or messages retained by Facebook, Instagram or X. Use the relevant platform's controls and permissions for platform-held information. Removing a local message is not a promise to unsend it or remove it from the recipient's account.
