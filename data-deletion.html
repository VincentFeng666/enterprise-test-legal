4. Disconnecting the Page

An internal administrator can open the application's account settings and select the Page's disconnect action (the current interface labels this action “断开”). It clears the account's stored access credentials, cache and OAuth states and cancels queued publishing jobs. If publishing is already in progress, disconnection is blocked until the operation finishes.

This action retains the account record, historical posts, comments, replies, metrics and audit or operational history. It is not a full deletion request. Use the contact channel above to request deletion of remaining records.

5. Removing authorization through Meta

You can also remove the application's authorization using the Facebook settings area where connected apps or business integrations are listed. Menu names vary by account and Facebook interface. Review Business Integrations or Apps and Websites as applicable.

Removing authorization does not automatically delete data already stored in enterprise-test. Submit a separate request for that data. If the Page is reconnected or synchronization continues, available Facebook content may be imported again; the operator must address that risk when carrying out the request.

6. Content on Facebook and completion

Deleting application-held records does not by itself remove a post, comment or reply from Facebook. Manage Facebook-hosted content using Facebook's own controls and applicable permissions. Meta controls the information retained by Facebook under its own policies.
