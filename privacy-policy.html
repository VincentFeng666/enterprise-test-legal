Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Operator and scope

enterprise-test is operated by Feng Siyang, an individual developer based in Guangdong, China. Contact: lei1294192563@163.com.

enterprise-test is a restricted development and testing tool for managing authorized Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and X accounts through their platform APIs. Access is limited to the developer and authorized testers. It is not a public account registration service. Only Pages that the operator or an authorized tester has permission to manage may be connected. If responsibility for operating the service transfers to a company, this policy will be updated to identify that company before its use under the new arrangements.

This policy covers internal account information, information received through authorized platform access, and information about people whose comments or private messages are processed by the application. It also covers the accompanying legal website.

2. Information processed

Depending on the functions used, the application processes:

  • Internal user names, email addresses, roles, account assignments, password hashes and session records.
  • Authorization information used to connect Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and X accounts, including platform account IDs and names, granted permissions, access credentials, renewal credentials where provided, expiry information and temporary authorization records. If several Pages are returned during authorization, their information is held for Page selection.
  • Page profile information, follower counts, post IDs, post text, publication times, links, comment IDs, commenter information returned by Meta, comment text and timestamps. Cached API responses may contain additional fields returned by Meta, including commenter identifiers.
  • Private-message conversation and participant identifiers (including Page-scoped identifiers where supplied), participant names or usernames where available, message identifiers, sender identifiers, message text, timestamps, incoming/outgoing direction and reply status for Facebook Messenger and Instagram. Related received Webhook events can contain message, postback, attachment, reaction or receipt information returned by Meta; not all event types are displayed or acted upon.
  • Platform media identifiers, source URLs, media types, dimensions, alternative text and other available media metadata for displayed posts or public interactions.
  • Uploaded media, drafts, schedules, review decisions, assignments, internal notes, replies and delivery status.
  • Available account and content analytics, including views or impressions, reach, engagement, likes or reactions, comments, shares, saves, reposts, follower changes, profile visits, website or post clicks, video metrics and aggregate audience information where returned. The application stores observed snapshots and derived report values and can export reports. Availability differs by platform and permissions; missing metrics are not assumed to have been collected.
  • Publishing and synchronization records, API usage information, errors, audit events and processing states for received platform events.

Platform data is received under the authorization granted to the application. Facebook and Instagram functions include content management, comments, available analytics and private-message receipt, bounded history synchronization and text replies when configured and enabled. Messages can arrive through Webhooks even without a user pressing the synchronization button. X functions cover authorized posts, public replies or mentions, displayed media and available analytics; this policy does not represent that X private messaging is implemented. Sandbox records are test records. Private-message attachment sending, complete history and complete read/delivery status support are not promised.

3. Purposes and authorization

Information is used to provide authorized Page management and testing functions: authenticate internal users, connect and select Pages, prepare and review content, publish and schedule approved content, read and respond to comments or private messages, organize conversations, display metrics, produce reports, and maintain operational records. Received events are held and processed to update authorized account records and prevent duplicate handling. A response cache reduces repeated API requests.

The Facebook authorization flow requests the permissions needed for the enabled functions. The default integration requests pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, pages_read_user_content and pages_manage_engagement, pages_messaging and read_insights. pages_messaging enables authorized Messenger functions; read_insights enables available Page analytics. Where Facebook Login for Business is used, permissions are supplied by its configuration. Availability depends on the permissions actually granted by Meta and the Page administrator. The application does not ask users to enter their Facebook password into enterprise-test.

Instagram uses Instagram API with Instagram Login. Requested permissions are instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_comments, instagram_business_manage_messages, instagram_business_content_publish and instagram_business_manage_insights, for account information, comment handling, messages, content publication and available analytics respectively. X authorization requests tweet.read, users.read, tweet.write, media.write and offline.access for authorized profile/post access, publication, media upload and continued authorization. Requested permissions do not imply that every permission or feature has been approved or enabled. Additional subscription-management permission, such as pages_manage_metadata, is used only when granted and required for the configured operation.

Where applicable law requires consent or another legal basis, the operator must establish it before the relevant processing begins. A Page administrator's API authorization does not by itself establish consent from every commenter or message participant. Private messages are processed to handle the conversation initiated with the authorized Page or professional account; a message does not authorize unrelated uses. Questions about applicable processing grounds, objection or consent withdrawal can be raised through the contact email. Where a required ground cannot be established, the affected processing must not proceed.

4. Storage, recipients and safeguards

The cloud application is hosted on Tencent Cloud infrastructure in Hong Kong. The operator is based in Guangdong, China. Hosting and authorized access may involve processing across jurisdictions. Applicable transfer requirements must be addressed before any processing that requires them.

Meta receives Facebook and Instagram authorization and API requests, including submitted posts, media, comments and private-message replies. X receives corresponding authorization and API requests for its enabled account functions. Published posts and public replies can be visible on the relevant platform according to its settings and rules. Private-message text is not published as a public post by this application; replies are sent to the relevant platform conversation. The platform and authorized account personnel may process or view those messages. Authorized users must not disclose private-message content through public posts or reports without a separate lawful basis and appropriate authorization. Tencent Cloud provides hosting infrastructure. Authorized personnel can access application records as needed for their permitted functions. X's own processing is described in its Privacy Policy. Additional providers or materially different uses must be assessed and disclosed before they are introduced.

The legal website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no application-added analytics scripts, advertising, forms or tracking cookies. GitHub may process website request information under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement Meta's own processing is described at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

The operator will restrict access to the application, storage, credentials and backups to authorized persons and use appropriate safeguards for the data processed. No security certification or guarantee of absolute security is represented by this policy.

5. Retention

The rule for active application records is to retain only information needed for authorized testing or unresolved operational work. The operator will review stored records at least every 30 days. This review includes private conversations, individual messages, raw event records, processing queues, caches, exported reports and media copies, as well as other application records. Records no longer needed will be removed within 30 days of that determination; when a testing activity ends, its associated records will be reviewed and unnecessary records removed within 30 days. Retention for future production use must be reviewed before that use begins.

Access credentials are removed through the relevant platform-account disconnection controls. The operator separately reviews any related authorization state and stored event information. Disconnection does not itself delete historical records. Temporary record validity or cache freshness is not a promise that database rows are automatically erased.

Backups will be retained for no more than 180 days from their creation and then removed through an operator-managed cleanup process. This is a maximum backup period, not a retention period for all active records and not a claim that the application performs automatic deletion. Unnecessary exported copies will be removed alongside the relevant active records.

If applicable law requires particular information to be retained, only the necessary information will be kept for the required period, with restricted access. The operator will explain the reason and period to the requester unless legally prohibited.

6. Requests and deletion

You may email lei1294192563@163.com to ask about information concerning you or request access, correction or deletion. You do not need an enterprise-test account to request deletion of your comments or private-message information. Include the platform, relevant account or Page, and a post/comment link or ID, or the approximate conversation time and your platform username where available. Do not send additional message content unless needed to identify the requested records. Do not send passwords, App Secrets, API keys or access tokens.

Feng Siyang handles requests personally. Receipt will be acknowledged within 7 days. After proportionate identity and data-scope verification, requests will normally be completed within 30 days, or sooner where applicable law requires. If more information is needed or a request cannot be fulfilled in full, the operator will explain the reason and applicable next steps. Status enquiries can be sent to the same email address.

Deletion is an operator-managed process, not an automated self-service feature. Relevant active records, conversations, messages, received raw events and queued processing work, cached information, media and exported copies will be assessed. The operator pauses affected synchronization and incoming-event processing as necessary and removes related queued events or prevents their reprocessing before activity resumes. Other participants' unrelated information is not deleted merely because it appears in the same account. Where selective removal from a backup is not feasible, the affected information will be restricted from ordinary business use until the backup expires, no later than 180 days from backup creation. Deletion must be reapplied before restored information is used. The operator will take steps to prevent deleted comments or messages from being reimported through history synchronization, delayed events, retries or restoration. Where reliable exclusion cannot be guaranteed, the affected collection or event processing remains paused.

Removing authorization in the relevant platform or disconnecting an account does not automatically erase historical application records. Deleting application records does not itself remove content or messages retained by the platform; platform-held information must be managed using that platform's own controls and applicable permissions.
