Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

1. Operator and scope

enterprise-test is operated by Feng Siyang, an individual developer based in Guangdong, China. Contact: lei1294192563@163.com.

enterprise-test is a restricted development and testing tool for managing Facebook Pages through the Facebook Graph API. Access is limited to the developer and authorized testers. It is not a public account registration service. Only Pages that the operator or an authorized tester has permission to manage may be connected. If responsibility for operating the service transfers to a company, this policy will be updated to identify that company before its use under the new arrangements.

This policy covers internal account information, information received through authorized Facebook access, and information about people whose comments are processed by the application. It also covers the accompanying legal website.

2. Information processed

Depending on the functions used, the application processes:

  • Internal user names, email addresses, roles, account assignments, password hashes and session records.
  • Authorization information used to connect Facebook Pages, including Page IDs and names, access credentials and temporary authorization records. If several Pages are returned during authorization, their information is held for Page selection.
  • Page profile information, follower counts, post IDs, post text, publication times, links, comment IDs, commenter information returned by Meta, comment text and timestamps. Cached API responses may contain additional fields returned by Meta, including commenter identifiers.
  • Uploaded media, drafts, schedules, review decisions, assignments, internal notes, replies and delivery status.
  • Engagement counts, metric snapshots, publishing and synchronization records, API usage information, errors and audit events.

Only information made available by Meta under the authorization granted to the application is received through its Facebook integration. The integration supports Page posts, top-level comment handling and selected metrics when configured and enabled. Live Facebook Messenger reading and sending are not currently implemented. Sandbox records are test records.

3. Purposes and authorization

Information is used to provide authorized Page management and testing functions: authenticate internal users, connect and select Pages, prepare and review content, publish and schedule approved content, read and respond to comments, display metrics, and maintain operational records. A response cache reduces repeated API requests.

The Facebook authorization flow requests the permissions needed for the enabled functions. The default integration requests pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, pages_read_user_content and pages_manage_engagement. Where Facebook Login for Business is used, permissions are supplied by its configuration. Availability depends on the permissions actually granted by Meta and the Page administrator. The application does not ask users to enter their Facebook password into enterprise-test.

Where applicable law requires consent or another legal basis, the operator must establish it before the relevant processing begins. A Page administrator's API authorization does not by itself establish consent from every person who comments on a Page.

4. Storage, recipients and safeguards

The cloud application is hosted on Tencent Cloud infrastructure in Hong Kong. The operator is based in Guangdong, China. Hosting and authorized access may involve processing across jurisdictions. Applicable transfer requirements must be addressed before any processing that requires them.

Meta receives authorization and Graph API requests, including submitted posts, media and replies. Published content can be visible on Facebook according to its settings and rules. Tencent Cloud provides hosting infrastructure. Authorized personnel can access application records as needed for their permitted functions. Additional providers or materially different uses must be assessed and disclosed before they are introduced.

The legal website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no application-added analytics scripts, advertising, forms or tracking cookies. GitHub may process website request information under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement Meta's own processing is described at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

The operator will restrict access to the application, storage, credentials and backups to authorized persons and use appropriate safeguards for the data processed. No security certification or guarantee of absolute security is represented by this policy.

5. Retention

The rule for active application records is to retain only information needed for authorized testing or unresolved operational work. The operator will review stored records at least every 30 days. Records no longer needed will be removed within 30 days of that determination; when a testing activity ends, its associated records will be reviewed and unnecessary records removed within 30 days. Retention for future production use must be reviewed before that use begins.

Access credentials will be removed when the corresponding Page is disconnected. Disconnection does not itself delete historical records. Temporary record validity or cache freshness is not a promise that database rows are automatically erased.

Backups will be retained for no more than 180 days from their creation and then removed through an operator-managed cleanup process. This is a maximum backup period, not a retention period for all active records and not a claim that the application performs automatic deletion. Unnecessary exported copies will be removed alongside the relevant active records.

If applicable law requires particular information to be retained, only the necessary information will be kept for the required period, with restricted access. The operator will explain the reason and period to the requester unless legally prohibited.

6. Requests and deletion

You may email lei1294192563@163.com to ask about information concerning you or request access, correction or deletion. You do not need an enterprise-test account to request deletion of your comment information. Include a relevant Page name or ID and a post or comment link or ID where available. Do not send passwords, App Secrets, API keys or access tokens.

Feng Siyang handles requests personally. Receipt will be acknowledged within 7 days. After proportionate identity and data-scope verification, requests will normally be completed within 30 days, or sooner where applicable law requires. If more information is needed or a request cannot be fulfilled in full, the operator will explain the reason and applicable next steps. Status enquiries can be sent to the same email address.

Deletion is an operator-managed process, not an automated self-service feature. Relevant active records, cached information, media and exported copies will be assessed. Where selective removal from a backup is not feasible, the affected information will be restricted from ordinary business use until the backup expires, no later than 180 days from backup creation. Deletion must be reapplied before restored information is used. The operator will take steps to prevent deleted interaction data from being reimported through continued synchronization.

Removing authorization in Facebook or disconnecting a Page does not automatically erase historical application records. Deleting application records does not itself remove content hosted on Facebook; Facebook content must be managed using Facebook's own controls and applicable permissions.

7. Changes and questions

The operator will publish updated policies on the legal website with a revised effective date. Material changes affecting authorized users will be communicated before they take effect where required. Questions can be sent to lei1294192563@163.com. Mandatory rights and remedies under applicable law remain available.
